## Jacksonville Electrician Market Overview

Jacksonville's unique combination of major port operations, naval installations, and healthcare creates diverse opportunities for electricians. The city's strategic location and military presence provide economic stability, while rapid growth drives construction demand.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $13-16/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $17-21/hour
- Annual range: $27,000-$44,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $22-29/hour
- With overtime: $46,000-$64,000 annually
- Military/port premium: +15-20%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $61,000-$76,000
- Project management: $67,000-$82,000
- Business ownership: $79,000-$110,000+
