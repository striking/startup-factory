## Oklahoma City Electrician Market Overview

Oklahoma City's energy sector dominance creates steady demand for skilled electricians, particularly in oil & gas, power generation, and aerospace industries. The city's low cost of living combined with competitive wages offers excellent purchasing power for electrical professionals.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $13-16/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $18-22/hour
- Annual range: $27,000-$46,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $23-30/hour
- With overtime: $48,000-$66,000 annually
- Energy/aerospace premium: +15-20%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $62,000-$77,000
- Project management: $68,000-$84,000
- Business ownership: $80,000-$110,000+

### High-Demand Specializations

**Energy Sector Electricians**
- Oil refineries: $32-38/hour
- Natural gas facilities: $30-36/hour
- Power plants: $34-40/hour

**Aerospace Industry**
- Aircraft maintenance: $28-34/hour
- Manufacturing facilities: $26-32/hour
- Testing/research: $30-36/hour

**Government/Military**
- Federal facilities: $26-32/hour
- Tinker AFB projects: $28-34/hour
- Security installations: $30-36/hour
