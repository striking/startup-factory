## Tampa Electrician Market Overview

Tampa's rapid growth as Florida's Gulf Coast economic hub creates exceptional demand for electricians across healthcare, tourism, port operations, and residential construction. The area's year-round construction season and population boom provide consistent, well-paying opportunities.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $13-16/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $18-22/hour
- Annual range: $27,000-$46,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $23-30/hour
- With overtime: $48,000-$66,000 annually
- Healthcare/industrial premium: +15-20%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $63,000-$78,000
- Project management: $69,000-$85,000
- Business ownership: $82,000-$115,000+

### High-Demand Specializations

**Healthcare Electricians**
- Hospital systems: $28-34/hour
- Cancer treatment facilities: $30-36/hour
- Medical research: $32-38/hour

**Tourism/Entertainment**
- Theme parks: $26-32/hour
- Hotels/resorts: $24-30/hour
- Convention centers: $28-34/hour

**Port/Maritime**
- Cargo facilities: $30-36/hour
- Ship maintenance: $32-38/hour
- Cruise terminals: $26-32/hour
