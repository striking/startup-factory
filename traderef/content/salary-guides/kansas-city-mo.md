## Kansas City Electrician Market Overview

Kansas City's position as America's transportation and logistics hub creates steady demand for electricians across rail, trucking, and distribution industries. The city's diverse manufacturing base and strong union presence provide competitive wages in a low-cost living environment.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $14-17/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $19-23/hour
- Annual range: $29,000-$48,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $24-31/hour
- With overtime: $50,000-$68,000 annually
- Industrial/rail premium: +15-20%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $65,000-$80,000
- Project management: $71,000-$87,000
- Business ownership: $86,000-$120,000+

### High-Demand Specializations

**Transportation/Rail Electricians**
- Railway systems: $32-38/hour
