## Atlanta Electrician Market Overview

Atlanta's position as the Southeast's economic capital creates diverse opportunities for electricians across multiple high-growth sectors. From massive film production facilities to corporate headquarters, the market offers steady work and competitive wages in a business-friendly environment.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $14-17/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $19-23/hour
- Annual range: $29,000-$48,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $25-32/hour
- With overtime: $52,000-$70,000 annually
- Specialized sectors: +10-20% premium

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $68,000-$82,000
- Project management: $74,000-$88,000
- Business ownership: $90,000-$125,000+

### High-Demand Specializations

**Entertainment/Film Industry**
- Studio electrical work: $30-38/hour
- On-location filming: $35-45/hour
- Permanent installations: $28-34/hour

**Healthcare Facilities**
- Hospital projects: $28-34/hour
- Emergency power systems: $30-36/hour
- Medical equipment installation: $32-38/hour

**Commercial Construction**
- Office buildings: $26-32/hour
- Retail centers: $24-30/hour
- Mixed-use developments: $28-34/hour
