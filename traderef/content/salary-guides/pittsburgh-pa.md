## Pittsburgh Electrician Market Overview

Pittsburgh's economic transformation from steel manufacturing to healthcare, technology, and education creates diverse opportunities for electricians. The city's strong union presence ensures competitive wages, while the low cost of living provides excellent purchasing power.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $15-18/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $20-24/hour
- Annual range: $31,000-$50,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $26-33/hour
- With overtime: $54,000-$74,000 annually
- Healthcare/tech premium: +12-18%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $70,000-$85,000
- Project management: $76,000-$92,000
- Business ownership: $92,000-$125,000+

### High-Demand Specializations
