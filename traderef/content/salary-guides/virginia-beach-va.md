## Virginia Beach Electrician Market Overview

Virginia Beach's massive military presence and shipbuilding industry create exceptional demand for skilled electricians specializing in naval, aerospace, and maritime electrical systems. The Hampton Roads region offers some of the nation's highest-paying specialized electrical work.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $15-18/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $20-25/hour
- Annual range: $31,000-$52,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $26-34/hour
- With overtime: $54,000-$76,000 annually
- Military/shipyard premium: +20-30%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $70,000-$86,000
- Project management: $78,000-$95,000
- Business ownership: $93,000-$130,000+

### High-Demand Specializations

**Naval/Shipyard Electricians**
- Aircraft carrier construction: $38-45/hour
- Submarine electrical: $40-48/hour
- Ship maintenance: $35-42/hour

**Military Installation**
- Base construction: $30-36/hour
- Aircraft maintenance: $32-38/hour
- Communications systems: $34-40/hour

**Aerospace/Defense**
- Fighter aircraft: $36-42/hour
- Weapons systems: $38-45/hour
- Radar/electronics: $35-41/hour
