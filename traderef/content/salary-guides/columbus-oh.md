## Columbus Electrician Market Overview

Columbus combines state capital stability with diverse economic sectors including education, healthcare, and insurance, creating steady demand for skilled electricians. The presence of major institutions and corporate headquarters provides consistent work opportunities with competitive wages.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $15-18/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $20-24/hour
- Annual range: $31,000-$50,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $26-33/hour
- With overtime: $54,000-$74,000 annually
- Government/institutional premium: +10-15%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $68,000-$84,000
- Project management: $74,000-$91,000
- Business ownership: $90,000-$125,000+
