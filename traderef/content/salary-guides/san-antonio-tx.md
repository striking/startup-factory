## San Antonio Electrician Market Overview

San Antonio's unique blend of military installations, aerospace manufacturing, and healthcare creates stable, diverse opportunities for electricians. The city's military presence provides recession-resistant work, while growing manufacturing and healthcare sectors drive expansion.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $14-17/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $19-23/hour
- Annual range: $29,000-$48,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $25-32/hour
- With overtime: $52,000-$71,000 annually
- Military/aerospace premium: +15-20%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $67,000-$83,000
- Project management: $73,000-$90,000
- Business ownership: $89,000-$125,000+
