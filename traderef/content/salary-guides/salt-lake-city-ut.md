## Salt Lake City Electrician Market Overview

Salt Lake City's unique position as the Mountain West's economic center creates exceptional opportunities for electricians, particularly in mining, technology, and aerospace industries. The city's business-friendly environment and strong industrial base provide stable, high-paying electrical work.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $16-19/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $21-25/hour
- Annual range: $33,000-$52,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $27-34/hour
- With overtime: $56,000-$76,000 annually
- Mining/industrial premium: +15-25%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $72,000-$88,000
- Project management: $80,000-$98,000
- Business ownership: $95,000-$135,000+

### High-Demand Specializations

**Mining Industry Electricians**
- Copper mine operations: $35-42/hour
- Processing facilities: $32-38/hour
- Heavy equipment maintenance: $34-40/hour

**Technology Sector**
- Data center construction: $30-36/hour
- Clean room facilities: $32-38/hour
- Server farm maintenance: $34-40/hour

**Aerospace/Defense**
- Manufacturing facilities: $30-36/hour
- Testing facilities: $32-38/hour
- Security installations: $28-34/hour
