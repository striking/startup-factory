## Nashville Electrician Market Overview

Nashville's explosive growth across healthcare, entertainment, and manufacturing creates exceptional opportunities for electricians. The city's business-friendly environment and lack of state income tax make it increasingly attractive to major employers, driving sustained electrical work demand.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $14-17/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $18-22/hour
- Annual range: $29,000-$46,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $24-30/hour
- With overtime: $50,000-$67,000 annually
- Healthcare/industrial premium: +12-18%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $65,000-$79,000
- Project management: $71,000-$86,000
- Business ownership: $85,000-$115,000+

### High-Demand Specializations

**Healthcare Electricians**
- Hospital construction: $28-34/hour
- Medical equipment installation: $30-36/hour
- Emergency power systems: $32-38/hour

**Entertainment Venue Electricians**
- Concert halls/arenas: $26-32/hour
- Recording studios: $28-34/hour
- Music venue installations: $30-36/hour

**Automotive Industry**
- Manufacturing facilities: $28-34/hour
- Assembly line systems: $30-36/hour
- Maintenance operations: $26-32/hour
