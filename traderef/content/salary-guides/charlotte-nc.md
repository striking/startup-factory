## Charlotte Electrician Market Overview

Charlotte's position as a major financial center drives significant commercial construction and infrastructure development, creating strong demand for skilled electricians. The city's diverse economy and business-friendly environment provide stable opportunities across multiple sectors.

### Salary Breakdown by Experience

**Apprentice Electricians (0-4 years)**
- Starting wage: $14-17/hour
- Mid-apprentice: $19-23/hour
- Annual range: $29,000-$48,000

**Journeyman Electricians (4+ years)**
- Base rate: $24-31/hour
- With overtime: $50,000-$69,000 annually
- Financial sector premium: +12-18%

**Master Electricians**
- Supervisory roles: $66,000-$81,000
- Project management: $72,000-$88,000
- Business ownership: $87,000-$120,000+
