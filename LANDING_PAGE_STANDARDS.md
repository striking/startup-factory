# Landing Page Standards

Guidelines for generated landing pages to maintain credibility while maximizing conversion.

## Truth Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

### ❌ NEVER Include
- Fake investor/VC backing ("Backed by Sequoia", "Y Combinator '26")
- Fabricated press mentions ("As seen on TechCrunch")
- Fake partnership logos
- Made-up statistics without basis ("10,000 users", "5M saved")
- False accreditations or certifications

### ✅ ALLOWED (Illustrative)
- Example testimonials (clearly from hypothetical users, no real names with fake quotes)
- Feature descriptions (what the product *would* do)
- Pricing tiers (aspirational)
- "Join X others on the waitlist" (if tracking real signups)
- FAQ answering hypothetical questions

## Realism Check

Before generating a landing page, the value proposition must pass these tests:

### The "Actually How" Test
Can you explain, step by step, how a real user would:
1. Discover the product
2. Complete the core action
3. Get the promised value

If any step is hand-wavy ("AI figures it out"), the value prop needs work.

### The "Would I Pay" Test
- Is this a real pain point?
- How much time/money does it cost the user today?
- Would they pay $29/mo to solve it?

### The "Workflow Reality" Test
Example failure: "Snap a photo of your work, get an invoice instantly"

Reality check for electrician installing ceiling fans:
- Work is hidden (wiring in walls)
- Photo doesn't capture labor time
- Photo doesn't show materials used
- Multiple rooms = fragmented workflow

Better framing: "Track time and materials as you work. Generate invoices in one tap."

## Required Sections

Every landing page must include:

1. **Hero** - Clear value prop, primary CTA (email capture)
2. **How It Works** - 3-4 realistic steps
3. **Features** - What the product does (can be aspirational)
4. **Social Proof** - Testimonials (marked as illustrative) OR "Join the waitlist"
5. **Pricing** - Tiers or "Free during beta"
6. **FAQ** - Common objections addressed
7. **Footer** - Privacy, Terms, Contact

## Beta/Waitlist Framing

All pages should use "beta" or "waitlist" language:
- "Join the private beta"
- "Get early access"
- "Be first to try [Product]"
- "Limited spots available"

This is honest (we ARE validating) and creates urgency.

## Social Proof Alternatives (No Fake Backing)

Instead of fake VC logos, use:
- "Join X tradies on the waitlist" (real count)
- "Built by [real background]" (e.g., "Built by a licensed electrician")
- Problem statistics (if sourced): "Australian tradies spend 5+ hours/week on admin"
- Industry context: "The trades industry loses $X billion to unpaid invoices"

## Design Standards

- Mobile-first (trades work on phones)
- Dark mode optional
- Fast loading (no heavy images)
- Accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Single CTA per section
- Email capture above fold

## Metrics Tracking

Every landing page must support:
- Email capture (waitlist)
- UTM parameter handling
- Basic analytics (page views, time on page)
- Conversion tracking (signup rate)
